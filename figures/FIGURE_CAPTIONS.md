# Figure Captions

## Figure 1. ThaiVoice-GUIBench Pipeline

Pipeline for the original 20-screen ThaiVoice-GUIBench controlled diagnostic benchmark. The top row shows screen, command, TTS, and ASR construction. ASR output then separates into a blue raw-grounding lane and an amber/teal fixed-correction lane; both converge at grounding evaluation, while transcript evaluation remains a separate endpoint. The three fixed methods are A no-screen cleanup, B screen string matching, and C full screen-aware alias correction. Transcript evaluation reports CER, Thai-tokenized WER, loanword error, recovery, and false correction rates; grounding evaluation reports raw and corrected-run click accuracy and changed-versus-unchanged analyses. Raw and corrected grounding are independent inference runs, so corrected-versus-raw grounding differences may contain independent rerun variability, as demonstrated by changed click outcomes for some unchanged prompts. Grounding prompts contain neither target element IDs nor target bounding boxes or gold answers.

## Figure 2. Per-Tier Correction Effects And False Correction Rate

Per-tier effects of the three fixed transcript-correction methods for Whisper large-v3 and Typhoon ASR on the original 20-screen benchmark. Blue circles denote A, vermillion squares B, and green triangles C. Panel A separates changed-transcript click effects into Whisper/G1, Whisper/G2, Typhoon/G1, and Typhoon/G2 facets; every point is labeled with its signed percentage-point effect and changed-subset denominator. Typhoon Methods A and B are shown as `n=0` transcript no-op controls, and the Typhoon/G1/C T3 result remains visible at $-100$ points with $n=3$. Panel B reports transcript-level false correction rates, shared across grounding models. Pooled corrected-versus-raw deltas are intentionally omitted from the figure and remain in Table 4 because they combine correction-related effects with independent grounding rerun variability; unchanged-prompt differences documenting that variability remain in Table 4b. Negative effects are not clipped, and no thresholds or correction settings were adjusted from this figure.
